SIMULATED PHISHING CASE

Scenario:

A customer receives an email claiming that their online banking
account will be suspended unless they verify their account
immediately.

The email contains the following URL:

http://secure-capitec-verification.example/login

This URL is a fictional training example and does not represent
a real Capitec website.

Initial Investigation:

Protocol: HTTP
Domain: secure-capitec-verification.example
Path: /login

Potential Warning Signs:

1. The message creates urgency by threatening account suspension.
2. The URL contains the brand name in a suspicious context.
3. The URL uses HTTP rather than HTTPS.
4. The URL contains a login page.
5. The domain is not an official banking domain.

Risk Assessment:

Risk Level: High

Reason:

The combination of urgency, brand impersonation, an account
verification request, and a suspicious domain are strong
indicators of a potential phishing attempt.
